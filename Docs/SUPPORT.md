# Månen Support

Månen is a minimal ambient soundscape app for iPhone.

## Common Questions

### The sound stops when I lock my phone

Make sure background audio is allowed by iOS and that the app was not force-quit from the app switcher.

### I cannot hear sound

Check the device volume, Silent Mode, connected Bluetooth devices, and whether another app is currently controlling audio playback.

### Do I need an account?

No. Månen does not use accounts.

### Does Månen collect data?

No. Månen does not collect personal data, use analytics, or track users.
