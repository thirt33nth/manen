# Månen Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Månen is designed as a private, offline ambient soundscape app.

## Data Collection

Månen does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not require an account, email address, phone number, location access, contacts access, calendar access, microphone access, camera access, or photo library access.

## Analytics and Tracking

Månen does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or cross-app tracking.

## Audio Playback

Månen plays audio files bundled with the app. Playback can continue in the background and on the Lock Screen using standard iOS media playback features.

## Network Access

Månen does not require network access for its core functionality.
